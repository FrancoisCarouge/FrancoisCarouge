## François Carouge

**Software leader building safe, fast, modern C++ for robotics and control systems.**

Fifteen years of leading large-scale distributed control and robotics software in regulated industries, from architecture and cybersecurity to field delivery. My open-source work brings type safety to physical computations.

### Projects

- **[Kalman](https://github.com/FrancoisCarouge/Kalman)**: Kalman filters for C++26: backend-agnostic, declarative, compile-time safe, public domain.
- **[TypedLinearAlgebra](https://github.com/FrancoisCarouge/TypedLinearAlgebra)**: strongly-typed  heterogenous linear algebra facade: backend-agnostic, zero runtime cost, compile-time safe, public domain.
- **Unreal Engine 4**: location and magnetic field [plugins](https://www.fab.com/sellers/Cerise%20Software).

### Experience

- **Johnson & Johnson**: Staff Software Engineer on the [Ottava](https://www.jnj.com/innovation/ottava-robotic-surgical-system) robotic surgical system: software infrastructure, quality, and cybersecurity.
- **NVIDIA**: [Isaac SDK](https://developer.nvidia.com/isaac): GPU-accelerated platform for building and deploying robotics applications.
- **Lutron Electronics**: technical lead on the [Quantum](https://www.lutron.com/us/en/controls/systems/quantum) commercial building control system, its largest-scale lighting and shading deployment, and the [myRoom](https://commercial.lutron.com/us/en/myroom) hotel integration.
- **Lehigh University**: teaching assistant in computer science and engineering.
- **Safran**: manufacturing quality R&D engineer: process and quality improvement for production.
